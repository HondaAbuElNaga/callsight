# ASR benchmark on our own calls

Gold set: 30 clips × 60 s (mono 8 kHz phone audio, both speakers); **30 / 30 typed**. Scored after `echonova-v1` normalisation, corpus-level WER/CER.
Generated by `python -m callsight goldset score` — do not edit by hand.

WER is strict (`echonova-v1`, comparable with the public table). *WER capped* cuts a word loop repeated more than 10 times to 10 copies (sada-paper rule) to show how much hallucination loops cost.
